# The tiles a level is made of. Each line: the character that stands for the tile in a level file, the tile's
# name (its picture is assets/tiles/<name>.png, 32 by 32), and what it is to the physics:
#
#   solid                        the whole square is ground: a floor on top, a wall at the sides, a ceiling below
#   slope <left> <right> <class> ground under a line: how many pixels down from the tile's top the surface is at
#                                the left edge and at the right edge (0 is the top, 31 the bottom), and how steep
#                                the slope it belongs to is: 1:1 (one up for one along, 45 degrees), 1:2 or 1:4.
#                                The class decides how hard the slope pushes downhill. It can be left out, and is
#                                then worked out from the two numbers.
#   oneway                       a platform: a floor to land on from above, nothing from below or from the side
#   decor                        drawn, and walked through
#
# In a level file '.' is empty air, 'P' is where the player starts and 'o' is something to pick up.
